Method

How a certificate is made

Plain arithmetic on public records. Nothing is estimated, and every figure links back to where it was read.

  1. Read the reserve figure

    For wrapped and bridged assets, the Chainlink Proof of Reserve feed on-chain (its latest round and timestamp). For USDC, USDT, PAXG, PYUSD, XAUT and USDe, the issuer's latest accountant's report or reserve dashboard.

  2. Read the supply, live

    The token's total supply on the chains we list, read about every two minutes, less known issuer treasury wallets. Each figure carries its chain, block and time.

  3. Weigh and date-stamp

    An issuer's verdict uses its report's own reserves and supply. A feed's verdict uses reserves over live supply. A feed past its heartbeat or a report past its limit is marked unverified.
