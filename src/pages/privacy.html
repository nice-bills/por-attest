Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

What we collect

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. The questions you type are sent to this service to produce an answer and are not stored by us. If the service answers a request routed through the Telegraph network, the network may keep the question and the answer as part of its signal record; see Telegraph's own policies.

Logs

The service runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes request metadata such as IP address and request path to deliver and protect the service, and short-lived request logs may be kept for debugging under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Fonts and scripts

Typefaces and scripts are served from this site. No third-party requests are made by the page.

Outbound reads

To answer, the service contacts public blockchain nodes and issuer websites. Nothing that identifies you is sent to them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's Telegraph hackathon submission.
